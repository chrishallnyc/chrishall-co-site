Sound credits

Raptor combines an edited engine recording with sounds created for the game. Engine power, airspeed, aircraft load, perspective, and nearby combat change the mix as you fly.

Afterburner texture

F-22 engine test in a hush house, March 10, 2020.
Recorded by Staff Sgt. Lucretia Cunningham, 192nd Wing, Virginia Air National Guard. DVIDS identifier: 200310-Z-PZ006-001.

The source is marked public domain by DVIDS. A short section of its audio was edited, filtered, and looped to support the game's afterburner sound. See the DVIDS use terms.

Sounds made for Raptor

Engine body and turbine layers, airflow and airframe vibration, cannon, missile motors, impacts, explosions, landing gear, wheels, brakes, and warning tones are authored for the game. These are an artistic interpretation; the warning tones do not reproduce operational aircraft systems.

The appearance of U.S. Department of War (DoW) visual information does not imply or constitute DoW endorsement.

Raptor is an independent project and is not affiliated with the aircraft manufacturer or the U.S. military.

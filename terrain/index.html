An atlas for exploring America through the shape of its landscape and the people who shaped its history.

Choose any of the 50 states. Get closer to its cities, follow its mountains and rivers, or switch on History to discover a collection of significant places.

Reading the atlas

History pins sit on today's landscape. Dates describe the story associated with a place. Guided journeys connect related places; their lines are reading paths, not surveyed historical routes.

Relief emphasizes height to make the terrain easier to read. Set it to 1× for the unexaggerated view. Use right-drag or two fingers to tilt and turn the map.

Sources & coverage

Terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles. Cities & places: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau. Imagery credits appear on the map. Read the atlas notes. Every historical place links to its own source.

Map imagery and elevation load over the internet. This is a historical and geographic explorer, not a navigation or land-survey tool.

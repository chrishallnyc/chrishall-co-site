Chris Hall is a New York City–born executive producer and media operator based in San Francisco. He is the producer of Y Combinator’s YouTube channel, which has nearly 2.5 million subscribers,[3] and has been Senior Video Producer at Y Combinator since January 2026.[1] He is also the founder of Hall Media, which builds media systems for founders, executives and investors.

Since 2020, through Hall Media, Chris has been executive producer for Garry Tan, now president and CEO of Y Combinator. In that period Tan’s YouTube channel grew from 20,000 subscribers to nearly 300,000.[1] He is also executive producer of The Social Radars, the podcast of Y Combinator co-founder Jessica Livingston and Carolynn Levy, creator of the SAFE.[2]

Early life and education[edit]

Chris was born in New York City. He studied cinematography and film and video production at the Academy for Careers in Television and Film from 2009 to 2012. As a student he was a director with Next Step Pictures, the production crew attached to the school, where he directed and operated camera on more than ten for-hire productions.

Career[edit]

Freelance work (2009–2019)[edit]

From 2009 to 2019 Chris worked in New York as an independent photographer, videographer and music producer, completing more than 220 projects. From 2012 to 2015 he was also Content & Social Media Specialist at the Young People’s Chorus of New York City, where he oversaw social media and internal video production and produced more than 30 videos as a one-person crew. He sang with the chorus and toured with it to Japan, China and across the United States.

Startups (2014–2019)[edit]

In 2014 Chris co-founded Kickster, which became the #1 sneaker app on the App Store and reached more than 200,000 monthly active users at its peak. In 2016 he was the architect of a Snapchat geofilter campaign with DJ Khaled that drew more than 300 million brand impressions. In October 2017 he co-founded Showroom Fashion Technologies, which helped some of the world’s largest influencers and brands sell high-quality merchandise that was closer to a fashion label than to “merch”. He was with the company until January 2019.

Hall Media (2019–present)[edit]

Chris founded Hall Media in March 2019. The company builds results-driven media systems for founders, executives, investors and high-net-worth individuals who need content made without unnecessary stress and exposure.

Hall Media’s longest-running client is Garry Tan, whose YouTube channel Chris has produced since March 2020. Chris is also executive producer of The Social Radars, the first podcast from Y Combinator co-founder Jessica Livingston and Carolynn Levy, who created the SAFE (simple agreement for future equity). The show interviews the founders of top Y Combinator companies. Other Hall Media work includes merchandise strategy for Ray Dalio’s Principles and how it tied into the brand’s content.

In 2021 Chris co-founded and built out Boerum Studios, a full-service commercial photo and video studio in East Williamsburg, Brooklyn. He was with the studio until 2023. Hall Media continues to take on client work alongside Chris’s role at Y Combinator.

Work with Garry Tan (2020–present)[edit]

Chris began producing Garry Tan’s YouTube channel in March 2020, when Tan was co-founder and managing partner of the venture firm Initialized Capital. Hall Media also worked with Initialized Capital from 2020 to 2023. Over the course of the collaboration Tan’s channel grew from 20,000 subscribers to nearly 300,000.[1]

Tan became president and CEO of Y Combinator in January 2023, and Chris continued as his executive producer. In May 2025 Tan brought Chris to San Francisco so that the two could work together in person. Chris joined Y Combinator full-time the following January.

Y Combinator (2026–present)[edit]

In January 2026 Chris joined Y Combinator full-time in San Francisco as Senior Video Producer. He produces the company’s YouTube channel, which has nearly 2.5 million subscribers.[3] His work with Tan, Livingston and Levy began through Hall Media, years before he joined the company.
